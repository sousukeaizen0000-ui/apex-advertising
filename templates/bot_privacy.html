Bot Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Apex Advertising may collect, use, store, and handle information when you interact with the Apex Advertising Discord Bot ("the Bot").

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the Bot's enabled features, information may include:

  • Discord user IDs and usernames.
  • Discord server and channel IDs.
  • Information submitted through Bot commands and forms.
  • Advertising submissions and promotion details.
  • Moderation records and staff activity logs.
  • Support requests and related messages.
  • Technical information needed to operate the Bot.

The exact information collected depends on the features enabled and how the Bot is configured.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Process advertising submissions.
  • Manage promotions and advertising activity.
  • Enforce community rules.
  • Support moderation and staff operations.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Maintain Bot security and functionality.
  • Diagnose errors and improve performance.

3. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be accessible to authorized Apex Advertising staff when necessary for moderation, support, administration, or Bot operation.

Information may also be processed by service providers required to host or operate the Bot.

4. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored using databases, hosting services, or other technical systems required for Bot operation. Reasonable measures are intended to protect stored information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary for operational, security, moderation, and support purposes. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the Bot's features.

6. Your Choices and Requests

If you have questions about information associated with your use of the Bot, you may contact Apex Advertising staff through the official support channels.

Requests to access or delete information may be reviewed, subject to legitimate operational, security, and legal requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

The Bot is intended for use in accordance with Discord's minimum age requirements and applicable laws. We do not knowingly seek to collect information from users who are not permitted to use Discord.

8. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and may rely on third-party hosting or infrastructure providers. Their own privacy policies and terms may also apply.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Bot develops. Updates will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Apex Advertising staff through the official support channels.

This is a starter policy draft. Before publishing, make sure it accurately describes the Bot's actual data collection, storage, sharing, and retention practices.
